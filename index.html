The problem

Job searching is broken.
We fixed it.

The average job seeker visits 5–6 websites, manually tracks applications in spreadsheets, and never gets closure from companies they applied to.

🗂️
Tab overload
LinkedIn. Indeed. ZipRecruiter. Glassdoor. You're burning hours switching between boards instead of applying.
📊
Spreadsheet hell
Manual tracking breaks down immediately. Applications get lost, follow-ups get missed, and you lose track fast.
👻
Company ghosting
You apply, hear nothing, and waste weeks wondering. No status updates, no rejection notice, no closure.
💸
Salary guessing games
Most postings hide the salary. You waste time interviewing only to find out the range was never in your ballpark.
💀
Ghost jobs
Filled roles stay posted for months. You apply to positions that were never open, wasting your time and energy.
🔍
No company intel
You can't tell which companies actually respond to candidates and which ones ghost everyone who applies.
